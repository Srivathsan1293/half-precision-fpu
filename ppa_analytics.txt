 5. SYNTHESIS NOTES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The original synth_sky130.ys script fails at the internal STA step with:
  "Module 'sky130_fd_sc_hd__*' is used with parameters but is not parametric!"
  Fix: Use separate passes for synthesis (yosys) and STA (OpenSTA).

- Created ppa_MUL.ys / ppa_ADDSUB.ys / ppa_DIV.ys for synthesis + area.
- Created sta_FMUL.tcl / sta_ADDSUB.tcl / sta_DIV.tcl for STA + power.

- FDIV's reciprocal_rom (1024x14) is blackboxed to avoid ABC exploding
  (~14k FFs from the memory map). The reported area/timing/power covers
  the control/datapath logic only.
