## Conclusion
Power reduction achieved by restructuring the correction tree from a 7-way if/else chain with 5 adders to parallel predicate encoding with single shared adder. The correction stage was the dominant power sink (~18% of FDIV power). Minor timing penalty (0.63 ns slack regression) acceptable for 18% power saving.
