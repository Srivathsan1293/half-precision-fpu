## P3.4 How good is this FPU?

### Against FPNew / CVFPU (FP16-slice, published)

- FPNew's fp32-wrap/half slice reports ~4-7 kGE for add+mul+div families at ~1 GHz-class targets in newer nodes; **this FPU is 2.0-5.5 kGE per unit (FMUL 2.0k, FADDSUB 1.5k, FDIV 5.5k)** in 130 nm, so per-unit gate cost is comparable to a same-node FP16 slice with a simpler FDIV.
- Sky130 FO4 ~50-90 ps: observed LTP is **172-327 FO4** (4-7.5 ns at 37.2 ps/FO4), i.e. ~120-160 MHz on tt/1.8V/25C. FPNew-style 1 GHz claims are not applicable at 130 nm; the fair comparison is per-FO4 depth, which is in the normal range for an RNE half-precision FPU.

### Against published Sky130 datapoints

- FP4/fp8 energy-optimized units: ~250 MHz, <0.01 mm2 (smaller datatypes, shallower trees). PicoRV32-class cores: ~100-200 MHz on Sky130. This FPU's **119-156 MHz (LTP bound)** on a *wider* half-precision data path is consistent with those numbers.
- fpu_test (33.2k um2 = 8.8k GE) is roughly **half the die devoted to FDIV (20.7k um2 / 57 pJ per DIV op)** - the dominant cost center, matching the "improve next" narrative.

### Verdict

Standalone-module quality is **middle-of-the-pack for 130 nm**: IEEE-754-exhaustive-correct (17.2e9 checks, 0 fails), ~100-160 MHz, 1.5-11 mW at 0.1 activity. The FP16/IPC metrics are competitive, while FDIV area+energy is the clear Pareto-gap item for any next revision.
